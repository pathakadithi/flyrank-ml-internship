Content Opportunity Scoring for Search-Driven Content Review

This project presents a decision-support approach for prioritizing pages for content review using observable historical search-performance signals from anonymized FlyRank data.

The capstone evaluates a Logistic Regression model trained to approximate a baseline-defined content-opportunity policy and validates the result using both random and client-grouped evaluation.

The analysis is intentionally framed as directional decision support. It does not claim to predict Google's ranking algorithm, future traffic recovery, or causal refresh impact.
